Joint surface membership — measured focal-plane coordinates

Drag to rotate; scroll to zoom. Hover for entry IDs and memberships. Click an event to highlight it in both planes. Click a legend item to hide a group; double-click to isolate it. With shared X or Y colors, legend visibility is linked between planes.

Click a point to inspect the same event in both focal planes.


The plots display saved memberships and never change a classification. Red/pink/gray crosses identify rejection categories. Display sampling deliberately preserves some rejection categories, so plotted density is not a population density estimate. Events with a nonfinite coordinate cannot be plotted; full counts still include them. No server, internet connection, or data upload is used.
